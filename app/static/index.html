Hotkeys

Enter / Shift+EnterNext / previous segment, ready to type
TabPlay / pause
Shift+TabReplay the segment from its start
EscLeave the text field

The keys below work on their own outside a text field. While typing, hold Alt.

1–9Assign speaker and mark it verified (the next free number adds a speaker)
VMark the speaker as verified, or undo that
RRelabel: give unverified segments the verified speaker they sound like
NNew segment at the playhead
[ / ]Set segment start / end to the playhead
SSplit at the playhead (and at the text cursor)
MMerge with the next segment
XDelete segment

Outside a text field only:

SpacePlay / pause
↑ / ↓Previous / next segment
← / →Seek 1 s (5 s with Shift)
+ / −Zoom (also Ctrl+scroll)
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+ZUndo / redo

Mouse: click the timeline to seek, drag to draw a segment, drag a segment edge to resize, double-click a segment to play it. Click a speaker name on the left to rename it.
